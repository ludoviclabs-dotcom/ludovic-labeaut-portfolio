Why this portfolio

A figure is only worth something if it can be proven.

That is the standard of the Master's in Accounting, Control and Audit. This portfolio applies it to six fields, from the most established to the newest: the method comes from the Master's, the subjects from today's world.

Six questions, six projects

Each question draws on a skill from the Master's. Under each one, the project that answers it.

01ProbantCore expertise

What the tool does
Inputs
The FEC general-ledger file, imported in a single drag and drop.
Checks
Every journal entry checked: duplicates, out-of-period entries, broken sequences.
Outputs
An audit-ready evidence file, sealed and time-stamped; the report, ready for the audit.
CCA requirement applied
Sufficient, appropriate audit evidence (NEP 500); completeness and cut-off; a compliant FEC (LPF, art. A47 A‑1) for accounts kept under French GAAP (PCG).
Master's DSCG units
  • UE4Accounting and audit
  • UE5Information systems management
View project Probant

02FinValStudioCore expertise

What the tool does
Inputs
Guided assumptions: cost of capital and computed WACC.
Checks
DCF (discounted cash flows, terminal value, bridge to equity value); peer multiples and median; Monte Carlo over 5,000 draws (P10, median, P90).
Outputs
Implied value per share; LBO: deleveraging, IRR and sponsor money multiple.
CCA requirement applied
Multi-criteria valuation (DCF, comparables) and sensitivity analysis of the assumptions.
Master's DSCG units
  • UE2Finance
View project FinValStudio

03Carbon&CoCore expertise

What the tool does
Inputs
Template workbook of activity data: energy, transport, purchasing, waste.
Checks
Scopes 1, 2 and 3 in tCO₂e (GHG Protocol method); double materiality of ESRS topics.
Outputs
CSRD report (ESRS 2, E1, G1) with audit trail; dashboard, alerts and projected trajectory.
CCA requirement applied
GHG inventory (GHG Protocol), double materiality (ESRS 1), sustainability information ready for limited assurance.
Master's DSCG units
  • UE4Accounting and audit
  • UE3Management and management control
View project Carbon&Co

04KYB GraphCore expertise

What the tool does
Inputs
26 public sources, via API or open data, never scraping.
Checks
Evidence quality of each link: confirmed, declared, inferred, simulated; explained risk signals, never a finding of fraud.
Outputs
SHA‑256 hash-chained log; PDF, JSON and ZIP pack export.
CCA requirement applied
AML/CFT due diligence: identifying the customer and the beneficial owner (French Monetary and Financial Code, art. L.561‑5 et seq.; Regulation (EU) 2024/1624, AMLR).
Master's DSCG units
  • UE1Legal, tax and social management
  • UE4Accounting and audit
View project KYB Graph

05PanoplieTopical field

What the tool does
Inputs
Filterable catalogue of systems; industrial graph: countries, systems, manufacturers.
Checks
Register of public costs: scope, year, uncertainty and sources; six grades from A to E, each one argued.
Outputs
System X-Ray and Evidence Pack in Markdown, per system.
CCA requirement applied
Full cost and traceability of every amount: scope, year, uncertainty.

06PublicationsTopical field

What the tool does
Inputs
A topic chosen from the series plan; dated, cross-checked sources.
Checks
Single sources flagged; map of the actors, their levers and who is exposed.
Outputs
A dated note: key points in four bullets, a six-section outline, published online.

Dissertation method: primary sources, cross-checking, dating.
